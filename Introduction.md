AI version

Recent advances in sequencing technologies, laboratory automation, and genome
assembly algorithms have opened the prospect that chromosome-level genome
assemblies may eventually become available for most known species. As genome
assemblies become increasingly abundant, their comparison is emerging as a
routine task for assembly quality control, variant discovery, evolutionary
analysis, and large-scale comparative genomics. Although a rich ecosystem of
specialised software exists for individual scientific questions, there remains
a need for a generic workflow that prioritises ease of deployment,
accessibility to non-specialists, reproducibility, robustness, and
high-throughput execution.

To address this need, we developed the \texttt{pairgenomealign} pipeline using the
Nextflow workflow system (\cite{Di_Tommaso_2017}) and the community standards,
reusable modules, and software-distribution ecosystem provided by nf-core
(\cite{Ewels_2020}; \cite{Langer_2025}).
