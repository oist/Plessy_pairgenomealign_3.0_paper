nf-core/pairgenomealign 3.0 publication
=======================================

Manuscript and supporting material of the article "nf-core/pairgenomealign: a
scalable pipeline for pairwise whole-genome alignment", by Charles Plessy,
Mohammed Mahdi, Takahiro Fujita, Martin C. Frith and Nicholas M. Luscombe.

Contents
--------

- `manuscript/`: the LaTeX manuscript, its bibliography and
  its figures, edited with Overleaf in the template of Peer Community In and
  Peer Community Journal.
- `manuscript/initial_drafts/`: notes and prompts used to
  write the first draft.
- `analyses/`: scripts and data supporting the article.
- `agent_prompts/`: prompts given to other AI agents, to compare their plans
  for a whole-genome alignment pipeline with the design of
  nf-core/pairgenomealign; not part of the article.
- `AGENTS.md`: instructions for AI agents working on this repository.

Releases of this repository are archived on Zenodo.
